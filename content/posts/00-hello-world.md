I started a little notebook on the internet.

I turned a year older on 14th April, and I randomly had the thought that maybe I should buy myself a domain. It felt like a nice idea.

Lately, I have been spending a lot of time on open source, Kubernetes, learning new stuff, and job hunting. It has been good, but at the same time it has also been mentally exhausting. Too many things to think about, too many things I want to figure out.

This had been in my backlog for a while.

I wanted a place where I could put things down without overthinking them too much. Things I am learning, things that excite me, random thoughts, things from life, or just something I do not want to forget later.

It took me some time to get used to how Hugo works, and setting everything up was honestly confusing and a lot of trial and error. But somehow it all came together into a working website, where all the links work, I hope.

Some posts might be technical.
Some might just be thoughts from a random evening.
Some might simply exist because I felt like writing them.
Some may just exist because they mattered to me in that moment.

I do not know exactly what this space will become yet.
Maybe notes, maybe stories, maybe technical deep dives, maybe just thoughts from long days.
For now, it is simply a place where I can put things down and come back to them later.

-Arnab

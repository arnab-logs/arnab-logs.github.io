Hello World, I am Arnab!

This is my little corner of the internet where I write about things I am currently interested in. I would like to think of this as a place where I grow ideas, curate thoughts and put things down before they slip away.

I’ll be honest, I’m not very good at sharing ideas. It doesn’t come naturally to me. Most of the time, I have a lot of things going on in my head but turning them into something that makes sense and putting them into words is a different story.

But I want to get better at it.

So I’m trying to spend a little more time sitting with what I learn, understanding things properly and writing them down. Maybe someone else will find something useful here or maybe I’ll come back months later and find something that reminds me of what I was learning back then.

Lately, a lot of that learning has been around open source. I’m currently contributing to a **Kubernetes subproject**, [node-readiness-controller](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/node-readiness-controller), and trying to understand everything around it i.e. how projects work, how people contribute and how much there is that I still don’t know.

Outside of that, I am into reading books, fiction and non fiction. You can read about my current reads [here.](https://app.thestorygraph.com/profile/arnabsreadingszn)

This is me trying to make sense of some of it.

As for this place, it’s still a work in progress, like most good things are...

Come in, look around.
